Yes, the University of Maryland (UMD) is currently test-optional. This policy has been extended through the Spring and Fall 2027 application cycles. This means that students applying for admission between now and Fall 2027 are not required to submit standardized test scores (SAT/ACT). Students have the option to decide whether or not to include their scores with their application.

UMD initially implemented its test-optional policy due to the limitations of testing in areas affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. The university is currently assessing the impact of this policy to inform its standardized test score requirement for application cycles beyond Fall 2027.

Here's a breakdown of what being test-optional means for different applicants:

For Freshman Applicants

  • Test Score Submission: You are not required to submit SAT/ACT scores. You can choose whether or not to include them.
  • Indicating Test-Optional Status: You will be able to indicate your test-optional status on your application.
  • Changing Status: Once you submit your application, your response to the test-optional question is final and cannot be changed.
  • Evaluation Without Scores: If you choose not to have your scores considered, the Admission Committee will not consider any submitted SAT/ACT scores in the application review. UMD uses a holistic, full-file review process, considering factors such as grades, academic trends, performance in advanced courses (Honors, IB, AP, A/AS level), letters of recommendation, essays, extracurricular activities, and other achievements.
  • Impact on Admissions: A decision not to submit SAT or ACT scores will not harm your admission chances.
  • Merit Scholarships and Special Programs: The review processes for merit scholarships, living-learning programs, and other special programs are also test-optional.
  • Limited Enrollment Programs (LEPs): Review processes for Limited Enrollment Programs are also test-optional.
  • Superscoring: If you choose to submit scores, UMD superscores both the SAT and ACT, using the highest subscores in their review. It is to your benefit to submit all your test scores if you choose to submit them.
  • Writing Portion and Subject Tests: UMD does not require subject tests or scores from the writing sections of the SAT or ACT. These scores are not among the 24 factors reviewed for admission, even if submitted.
  • English Language Proficiency: Standardized tests for English language proficiency (e.g., Duolingo English Test, IELTS, TOEFL) are still required for non-native English speakers.

For Transfer Applicants

  • Test Score Submission: UMD is currently test-optional for transfer students as well. Traditionally, transfer students with fewer than 30 semester or 45 quarter hours at the time of application would need to submit SAT and/or ACT scores, but this is no longer a requirement due to the test-optional policy.
  • Indicating Test-Optional Status: You will be able to indicate your test-optional status on your application.
  • Evaluation Without Scores: If you choose not to have your test scores considered, the Admission Committee uses a holistic, full-file review.
  • Superscoring: UMD superscores both the SAT and ACT, using the highest subscores from your submitted tests.

Important Considerations

  • Academic Year: This test-optional policy is in effect through the Spring and Fall 2027 application cycles.
  • Future Policy: UMD is currently assessing the impact of the test-optional policy to determine its future beyond Fall 2027.

For the most current and complete information, please refer to the Office of Undergraduate Admissions website.
